Welcome

I work with what makes us human — grief and loss, relationships of all kinds (romantic, family, friendships, work), questions of meaning, loneliness, time, and purpose.

If you're feeling scared, uncertain, anxious, or just... stuck — that's already enough reason to reach out.

In longer-term work, we go deeper — exploring patterns, reactions, and what drives them. With commitment from both of us, real and lasting change becomes possible.

Even considering therapy is a brave step. You're already looking inward.

"I walk beside you, not ahead. Together, we explore what it means to be fully alive in this moment."

My Approach

How I Can Support You

I offer both short-term and long-term therapy in the Gestalt tradition — meeting you exactly where you are, right now.

Long-Term Therapy

6+ months

For those ready to go deeper. We'll explore the patterns that shape your life and relationships. With time and trust, real transformation happens.

  • Personal growth
  • Understanding patterns
  • Relationship work
  • Lasting change

Short-Term Support

Up to 10 sessions

Sometimes you need focused support for something specific — a sudden change, a decision, a transition. We work with clarity and intention.

  • Something pressing
  • Life transitions
  • Focused goals
  • Clear timeline
