Welcome

I'm glad
you're here

I work with what makes us human — grief and loss, relationships of all kinds (romantic, family, friendships, work), questions of meaning, loneliness, time, and purpose.

If you're feeling scared, uncertain, anxious, or just... stuck — that's already enough reason to reach out.

In longer-term work, we go deeper — exploring patterns, reactions, and what drives them. With commitment from both of us, real and lasting change becomes possible.

Even considering therapy is a brave step. You're already looking inward.

"I walk beside you, not ahead. Together, we explore what it means to be fully alive in this moment."

About Me

Training & Background

I share what's professionally relevant and leave space for you to discover the rest through our work together.

Education

  • Diploma in Psychological Counselling (Saint Petersburg, Russia)
  • MSc in Bioethics (KU Leuven, Belgium)
  • MA in Philosophy (University of Tartu, Estonia)
  • Training at International Institute of Gestalt, Montenegro (ongoing)

Ethics & Practice

  • I work to the ethical standards published by the European Association for Gestalt Therapy (EAGT)
  • Weekly personal therapy and supervision
  • I welcome everyone — all backgrounds, identities, and ways of being

Working Alongside

What We Might Work On

None of these will describe you exactly — your story is your own. These are simply the doors people most often come through.

Grief & loss

Death of someone close · Anticipatory grief and long illness · Miscarriage and childlessness · Losing a home or country · The end of a relationship · Grief no one around you recognises

Relationships

Partners and intimacy · Repeating patterns in who you choose · Parents and family roles · Friendship, distance and drifting apart · Conflict and boundaries at work · Loneliness inside a relationship

Anxiety & feeling stuck

Constant low-level worry · Dread with no clear cause · Burnout and exhaustion · Procrastination and self-criticism · Shame and perfectionism · Anger you don't know where to put

Transitions & meaning

Emigration and life between countries · Career change or losing work · Becoming a parent · Ageing, time and mortality · Identity, values and belonging · "Is this really my life?"
