    Choose your session type

    Individual Therapy

    50 min
    ... is a one-to-one space to explore your thoughts, emotions, and experiences in a supportive and confidential setting. Using a Gestalt approach, the focus is on increasing awareness of your feelings, patterns, and relationships in the present moment. This type of session can help with personal growth, emotional difficulties, life transitions, stress, or a deeper understanding of yourself and how you relate to others.

    Relationship & Interpersonal Therapy

    50 min
    This session focuses on improving communication and understanding between people who share an important relationship. It can help address conflicts, clarify expectations, and find constructive ways to move forward. It is suitable for couples, friends, business partners, or individuals who want to reflect on and improve their relationships, communication, and decision-making in personal or professional contexts.

    Bioethical consultation

    50 min
    ... a session where we talk through difficult ethical questions related to health, medicine, or life decisions. These situations can arise when people face complex medical choices, disagreements about care, or uncertainty about what the most ethical or responsible decision might be. During the consultation, we carefully explore the situation, clarify the values and concerns of everyone involved, and discuss the ethical principles that may apply. The goal is not to tell you what to do, but to help you understand the ethical aspects of the situation and support thoughtful, informed decision-making. This type of session can be helpful for individuals, families, caregivers, or professionals who want guidance when facing challenging questions about medical treatment, consent, end-of-life decisions, research participation, or other ethically sensitive issues.
