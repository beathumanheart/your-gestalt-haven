    Nothing open yet

    Touch a ring on the map and it opens here.

    The order of the rings follows the emotion-focused sequence set out by Leslie Greenberg: attend to the body, put the feeling into language, tell a reactive feeling from the one underneath it, and find the need the feeling points at. Each feeling carries a bodily sense, an impulse, and a need.

    The map describes and asks. It does not interpret, diagnose or conclude anything about you — that work belongs to you and the person you sit with. It is built to sit beside step work, gestalt awareness practice and analytic counselling without standing in for any of them.
